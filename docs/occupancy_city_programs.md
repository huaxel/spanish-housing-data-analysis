# Can we compare affordable-rental mediation across four cities?

**Not with a single published count.** The sources below describe municipal
programs, not the provinces in the [stock pilot](occupancy_province_pilot.md).
An owner agreement, an active lease and a launch announcement are different
stages. “Not verified” means a suitable figure was not found in the sources
checked, not that the program produced zero leases.

| City and program | What the source measures | What it does not establish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Barcelona — social-rental mediation network | **176 dwellings rented during 2025; 1,301 with an active contract at year-end**, from the Generalitat’s municipal tables. | The network figure excludes cessions managed by the Catalan Housing Agency. It is not a count of previously empty homes or all assisted leases. |
| Madrid — EMVS ReViVa | **200 homes incorporated**, announced `2026-01-26`. | Owners can be paid whether or not a tenant has signed. This is not a lease count or a calendar-2025 intake figure. |
| Alicante — El Claustro | **539 active contracts** in the `2026-04-11` announcement; **159 new contracts since the service was renewed in 2024**. | The second number is cumulative through the announcement, not a 2025 flow. Neither number supplies a comparable Barcelona-style 2025 year-end observation. |
| València — AUMSA municipal rental agency | The city approved ending Reviure in 2023. A separate agency launched `2026-07-17`. | The launch announcement gives terms and objectives, not fulfilled leases. No comparable program outcome was verified for 2025; this is not a zero count of ongoing legacy contracts or other programs. |

## Source trail

- Barcelona: [published workbooks and reproduction](occupancy_barcelona_mediation.md).
- Madrid: [city announcement](https://diario.madrid.es/blog/notas-de-prensa/el-programa-reviva-del-ayuntamiento-alcanza-las-200-viviendas-vacias-recuperadas-para-el-alquiler-asequible/)
  and the accessible [Telemadrid account of the same announcement](https://www.telemadrid.es/programas/telenoticias-1/El-programa-ReViVa-alcanza-las-200-viviendas-recuperadas-para-alquiler-asequible-en-Madrid-2-2856334368--20260126030851.html).
  The latter confirms the milestone and confirms that owners are paid even
  when the home is not rented. It is not a separate data collection.
- Alicante: [city announcement](https://www.alicante.es/es/noticias/alicante-gestiona-539-viviendas-privadas-alquiler-asequible-pisos-desocupados)
  and the accessible [Radio Alicante account quoting the city](https://cadenaser.com/comunitat-valenciana/2026/04/11/alicante-gestiona-539-viviendas-privadas-en-regimen-de-alquiler-asequible-para-poner-en-uso-pisos-desocupados-radio-alicante/).
  The figures are attributed to that announcement; the two reports do not
  constitute independent records of contracts.
- València: [municipal decision ending Reviure](https://www.valencia.es/web/gobierno-abierto/cas/informacion-institucional/organizaci%C3%B3n/organos-de-gobierno/junta-de-gobierno/-/asset_publisher/34304344101/content/id/25019389)
  (agenda item `0028`, approved `2023-10-13`) and [AUMSA agency launch](https://www.valencia.es/es/-/agencia-municipal-del-alquiler-val%C3%A8ncia)
  (announced the day before opening). They are not two points on a continuous series.

## A common reporting request (draft, not sent)

> Solicito una tabla agregada por programa y año natural, limitada al
> municipio: viviendas distintas con un nuevo acuerdo con la propiedad;
> viviendas ofrecidas a solicitantes; viviendas cuyo primer contrato de
> arrendamiento en el programa se firmó en el año; viviendas con contrato
> vigente a 31 de diciembre; y salidas y renovaciones, por separado. Indiquen
> la fecha de corte, si se incluyen viviendas que ya estaban alquiladas al
> incorporarse, y cómo se tratan los contratos sucesivos en una misma vivienda.
> Distingan cero de dato no disponible. No se solicitan datos personales.

For a true intake-to-lease rate, request an additional aggregate by intake
cohort: homes admitted in the same calendar year and the number with a first
signed lease by a common follow-up date. Do not divide unrelated annual
figures, or use [survey-estimated off-market homes](occupancy_euskadi_euv.md)
as the program denominator. No request was sent and no figures were integrated
into the national mart.
