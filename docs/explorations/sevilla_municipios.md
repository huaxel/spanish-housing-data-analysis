Sevilla city holds 34.9% of the provincial population (689,423 in 2025 —
fewer than in 2011). The highest 2024 rents are Espartinas (9.67), the
capital (9.17) and Mairena del Aljarafe (8.44): the Aljarafe pulls as hard
as the capital.

## Vacancy 2011: campiña and sierras vs capital

Sevilla city: 48,178 vacant dwellings, 14.3% of its 2011 stock. The maxima
are inland — La Puebla de Cazalla (26.0%), La Puebla de los Infantes
(25.4%), Villanueva del Río y Minas (24.6%) — against the capital's 14.3%.
Same overhang geography as Valencia: empty interior, tight centre.
Descriptive only.
