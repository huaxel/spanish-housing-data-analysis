# Bizigune: from off-market homes to protected rental

The [Euskadi housing-use survey](occupancy_euskadi_euv.md) distinguishes
uninhabited homes already on offer from those off market. Bizigune measures
something different: homes whose owners grant use to a public operator for
protected rental. Alokabide offers rent and return-condition guarantees and
can help prepare dwellings for admission. The program’s management reports
separate the resulting stock from agreements signed in a given year.

| Measure | 2024 | 2025 |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Bizigune dwellings under management at year end | 7,375 | 7,475 |
| New usufruct agreements with owners during the year | 396 | 360 |
| Owner agreements falling due during the year | 1,452 | 1,150 |
| Of those, renewed | 1,172 | 887 |

The stock increased by **100** between the two year ends, not by the
**360** new agreements signed in 2025. Renewals maintain existing stock;
a new agreement is not a signed tenant lease. The reports do not supply a
complete dwelling-level reconciliation, so the difference is not an audited
count of homes that left the program.

**Tenant leases are a separate measure.** In Alokabide’s 2025 Bizigune
allocation table, **123 of 272** allocations ended in a signed rental
contract by the reporting date. This is a status count for the allocation
cohort, not the share of the year’s new owner agreements that reached tenants.
The portfolio includes homes from earlier years. Neither a stock count nor a
contract count proves that the program caused an equal net increase in the
region’s rental supply.

- The Basque Government’s [rental-market press release](https://www.irekia.euskadi.eus/mobile/es/news/112151-san-sebastian-irun-errenteria-registran-descensos-del-los-nuevos-alquileres-cierre-2025)
  instead reports **7,499** at the end of 2025, up **80**. We could not
  reconcile that count to the management reports. Use the paired Alokabide
  reports for the change above; do not mix endpoints across publications.

This case documents one program’s operations in Euskadi. Its portfolio is
not the denominator for an uptake rate, and dividing agreements by the
survey’s off-market stock would not yield one: the survey and program do
not track the same homes or the same dates. No program figures were added
to the national mart.
